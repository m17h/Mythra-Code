## Onboarding review plan

Three GPT-6 Sol reviewers at high effort reviewed feature accuracy, usability/accessibility, and behavior/performance independently. Reconciliation preserves the requested emphasis on personalization and flexible sub-agents while reducing nine pages to five:

1. Welcome and connect AI: five compact provider choices, one detail panel, direct setup action and accurate per-provider readiness.
2. Projects and chats: local folders first; optional GitHub/worktree/PR explanation.
3. Direct the work: permissions, stopping/questions, opt-in sub-agents with any available connected model.
4. Make it yours: clearly labeled, non-persisting theme/font/slider previews; automatic titles in Settings → Projects.
5. Get started: project/chat destinations; advanced tools grouped as optional exploration.

Required behavioral repairs: suspend and resume the same onboarding page across Settings; stay in onboarding when the folder picker is cancelled; don't treat unrelated credentials/runtime as a ready provider; don't steal arrow keys from inputs; focus the new page heading and keep footer actions reachable. Established installations must not be forced through onboarding again.
